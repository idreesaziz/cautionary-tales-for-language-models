The author licenses original code under MIT and the original corpus, paper, figures, and research data under CC BY 4.0. [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) records the grant and its scope. Existing upstream licenses apply to their respective material.

| Material | Source and terms |
|---|---|

| Emotion stories | `ryancodrai/emotion-probes`, pinned revision; CC BY 4.0. |

| Project-authored code | [MIT](../LICENSE). Retain the copyright and permission notice. |
| Project-authored corpus, paper, figures, and research data | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit the author, link the license, and identify changes. |

[Third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identify upstream papers and datasets. Download registries record byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Model access follows each provider's license and access requirements.

The intervention corpus contains synthetic accounts. Its [data card](../DATA_CARD.md) describes provenance, mechanical checks, and the scope of content review. Preserve those records when creating derived datasets.

The report's required ethical appendix describes targeted answer suppression through consequence stories. The [abstention audit](ABSTENTION_AUDIT.md) measures lost correct answers alongside error avoidance.
